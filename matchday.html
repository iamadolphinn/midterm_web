Saturday · 15:00 kick-off

Your matchday, hour by hour

From the train station to the final whistle. Follow the plan and you will not miss a thing.

11:00

Arrive in Liverpool

Get off at Lime Street station and have breakfast in the city centre.

12:45

Head to Anfield

Take the 917 express bus from near Lime Street (about 15 minutes), the Soccerbus from Sandhills station, or walk 3 km.

13:15

Walk around the stadium

See the Shankly Gates, the Paisley Gateway and the Hillsborough memorial.

14:00

Grab some food

Try a pie from a local bakery and soak up the atmosphere in the streets.

14:30

Find your seat

Go in early. Watching the stands fill up is part of the show.

14:55

The anthem

Scarves up. All 61,276 fans sing "You'll Never Walk Alone".

15:00

Kick-off

90 minutes of noise. Sing along with the Kop.

16:55

Final whistle

Special buses take fans back to the city centre after the game.

Before you buy

Good to know

Most home tickets go to club members through a ballot, so become a member before the season starts.

Junior and local tickets cost £9, and young adults aged 22 to 24 pay half the adult price.

Your ticket on your phone, a red scarf and a warm jacket. Leave big bags at home.
