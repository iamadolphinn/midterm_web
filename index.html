Before you go

Why Anfield is special

01

The Kop

One huge single-tier stand behind the goal. It is where the loudest songs start.

02

Shankly Gates

Put up in 1982 for the legendary manager Bill Shankly. Most fans take their first photo here.

03

The anthem

Just before kick-off the whole stadium sings "You'll Never Walk Alone" with scarves up.

04

The memorial

The Hillsborough memorial with its eternal flame remembers the 97 fans who lost their lives in 1989.
